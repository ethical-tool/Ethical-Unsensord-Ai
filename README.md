## 📖 About

**Ethical-Uncensored-AI** is a curated global directory for discovering AI platforms, open-weight models, local AI applications, and developer resources.

This project brings together useful AI resources in one organized place, making it easier to explore different platforms, compare available technologies, and discover tools for research, development, education, and creative work.

### What You Will Find

- Hosted AI chat platforms
- AI directories and model aggregators
- Local and offline AI applications
- Open-weight and community models
- AI model providers
- Developer APIs and integrations
- AI research and community resources

> **Disclaimer:** Not every listed platform is fully uncensored. Features, availability, pricing, privacy policies, and usage restrictions depend on individual providers.

## 🔒 Privacy, Security & Licensing

- **Privacy:** Review provider privacy policies before sharing personal or confidential information.
- **Hosted AI:** Cloud platforms may process your prompts on their servers.
- **Local AI:** Running models locally offers more control, but system security remains important.
- **Model Licensing:** Open weights do not automatically mean unrestricted commercial usage.
- **Verification:** Check official sources for current features, availability, and policies.
- **Responsible Usage:** Use AI technologies legally and responsibly for education, research, development, and creative projects.

## 📌 Project Status
